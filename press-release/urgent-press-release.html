Issued by: Adriana Venter — Partner for Microsoft | Blockchain Strategist | CEO, Signature Inc Software Solutions

Summary

Telemetry and preliminary evidence indicate suspected large-scale cloning, forged credentials, and unauthorized reseller activity that may be impacting critical systems and services. This situation is under active investigation by our incident response team and partnering law enforcement authorities.

Reported scope (under investigation)

Initial telemetry lists a range of systems and services under investigation. These are reported as suspected and remain subject to formal forensic confirmation.

  • Cloud consoles and admin exchanges (GoDaddy, Azure, Microsoft/Outlook Exchange, Google Console) — reported unusual redeployments
  • Multiple repo and product names reported in traces (e.g., Turbo, Terra Data, Blockstream, Jade suite, Titanium-branded assets)
  • Blockchain trace systems, medical remote management, police/RFID systems — reported anomalies

Immediate guidance

  • Do not buy enterprise licenses or services from unverified resellers — verify authorization with vendors.
  • Rotate admin credentials, enable multi‑factor authentication (MFA), and preserve logs if you suspect compromise.
  • Report confirmed abuse to vendor abuse contacts and local law enforcement.
